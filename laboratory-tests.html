Test Descriptions

CBC (Complete Blood Count)
CBC checks overall health and helps detect infections, anemia and other blood-related conditions.
HbA1c
HbA1c measures the average blood sugar level over the last 2-3 months.
LFT (Liver Function Test)
Evaluates the health and proper functioning of the liver.
KFT (Kidney Function Test)
Determines how efficiently the kidneys are filtering waste from the blood.
ECG (Electrocardiogram)
Records the electrical activity of the heart and helps identify certain heart-related abnormalities.
