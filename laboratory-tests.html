Test Descriptions

CBC (Complete Blood Count):
CBC checks the overall health and helps detect infections, anemia, and other blood disorders.
HbA1c:
HbA1c measures them average blood sugar level over the last 2-3 months.
LFT (Liver Function Test):
Evaluates the health and proper functioning of the liver.
KFT (Kidney Function Test):
Determines how efficiently the kidneys are filtering waste from the blood.
ECG (Electrocardiogram):
Records the electrical activity of the heart and helps detect heart diseases.
