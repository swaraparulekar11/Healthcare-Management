Clinical Laboratory Infrastructure

Our Clinical Laboratory is equipped with diagnostic facilities designed to provide reliable and timely test results. The laboratory maintains a clean, safe and patient-friendly environment.

Different areas are arranged for sample collection, testing, sample processing and report management. Proper handling of samples helps maintain the quality of laboratory work.

Facilities Available

  • Advanced Diagnostic Equipment
  • Automated Analyzers
  • Digital Report Management
  • Sample Storage Facility
  • Quality Control Laboratory

Main Areas of Laboratory

🩸 Sample Collection

A separate area is used for collecting blood and other samples from patients.

🔬 Testing Area

Samples are examined using suitable laboratory instruments and testing methods.

💻 Report Section

Test results can be organized and maintained through digital report management.

Safety and Sample Handling

Laboratory samples are labelled properly and handled carefully. Clean work areas, appropriate storage and basic safety practices are important parts of laboratory operations.
