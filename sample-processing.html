Sample Processing Area

The Sample Processing Area is responsible for receiving, labeling, storing, and preparing patient samples before laboratory analysis. Each sample is carefully handled to prevent contamination.

Laboratory technicians verify patient details, separate different sample types, and process them using standard laboratory procedures. Proper storage conditions are maintained until testing is completed.

Processing Steps

  1. Sample Verification
  2. Barcode Labeling
  3. Sample Separation
  4. Storage and Preservation
  5. Transfer for Laboratory Testing
