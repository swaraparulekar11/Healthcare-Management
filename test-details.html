Laboratory Test Details

CBC Blood Count:

Purpose:Detects infections, anemia, and other blood disorders.

Sample Required: Blood Sample

Preparation Instructions:No special preparation is required.

Normal Reference Range: WBC: 4,000-11,000 cells/µL

Report Delivery Time: Within 24 Hours

Important Instructions:
Inform the technician if you are taking any medication.

Fasting Requirement: Not Required

Consult your doctor to understand the report.

HbA1c Test

Purpose: Measures average blood sugar over the last 2-3 months.

Sample Required: Blood Sample

Preparation Instructions: No fasting is required.

Normal Reference Range: Below 5.7%

Report Delivery Time: Within 24 Hours

Important Instructions:
Carry previous diabetes reports if available.

Fasting Requirement: Not Required

Recommended for diabetes monitoring.

LFT (Liver Function Test)

Purpose: Evaluates liver health and function.

Sample Required: Blood Sample

Preparation Instructions: Avoid alcohol for 24 hours before the test.

Normal Reference Range: ALT: 7-56 U/L

Report Delivery Time: Within 24 Hours

Important Instructions:
Inform your doctor about any medicines you are taking.

Fasting Requirement: 8 Hours Recommended

Follow doctor's advice before testing.

KFT (Kidney Function Test)

Purpose: Assesses kidney function and detects kidney disease.

Sample Required: Blood Sample

Preparation Instructions: Drink sufficient water before the test.

Normal Reference Range: Creatinine: 0.6-1.2 mg/dL

Report Delivery Time: Within 24 Hours

Important Instructions:
Inform the laboratory about kidney-related medications.

Fasting Requirement: Not Mandatory

Results may vary with age and gender.

ECG (Electrocardiogram)

Purpose: Records the electrical activity of the heart.

Sample Required: No blood sample required.

Preparation Instructions: Wear comfortable clothing and remain relaxed.

Normal Reference Range: Heart Rate: 60-100 bpm

Report Delivery Time: Immediate

Important Instructions:
Avoid heavy exercise before the test.

Fasting Requirement: Not Required

ECG should be interpreted by a qualified cardiologist.
