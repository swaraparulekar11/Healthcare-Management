Blood Collection Services

Blood Collection Room

The Blood Collection Room is designed to provide a safe, hygienic, and comfortable environment for patients. Trained phlebotomists collect blood samples using sterile and disposable equipment.

Every sample is properly labeled and verified before being sent to the laboratory for testing. Strict infection control procedures are followed to ensure patient safety.

Services

  • Routine Blood Sample Collection
  • Fasting Blood Tests
  • Health Check-up Sample Collection
  • Home Collection Support
