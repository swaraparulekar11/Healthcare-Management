Blood Collection Room

About Blood Collection

The Blood Collection Room is a dedicated area where blood samples are collected from patients for different laboratory investigations.

Blood samples are collected using appropriate collection equipment and are properly labeled before being sent to the laboratory for further processing and testing.

Maintaining cleanliness and following proper sample collection procedures helps in handling patient samples safely and efficiently.

Important Steps During Collection

  • Verify the patient's details before collection.
  • Use appropriate collection equipment.
  • Collect the required sample carefully.
  • Label the sample correctly.
  • Send the sample to the laboratory for testing.

Patient Instructions

Patients should follow the instructions given for their particular test. Some blood tests may require fasting, while others may not.

If you are unsure about any preparation required before your test, please contact the laboratory before visiting for sample collection.
